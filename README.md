## 🤔 What Is tashev-proof?

Have you ever used an AI coding tool like Claude, Codex, or Cursor, and the AI said "I'm done!" — but you weren't really sure if it actually finished the job? 

**tashev-proof** is a simple tool that checks if AI-assisted work is truly complete. It creates something called "acceptance contracts" — think of them as a checklist that defines what "done" really means. Then, it collects real evidence to prove the work meets those standards. Finally, it gives you a clear verdict: **Ship it** or **Fix it**.

Think of it like a quality inspector for AI-generated code. You tell it what "done" looks like, and it verifies that the AI actually did what it claimed.

## 📖 How to Use tashev-proof

### First-Time Setup

When you first open tashev-proof, you'll see a welcome screen. Here's what to do:

1. **Create a new project** — Click "New Project" and give it a name
2. **Define your acceptance criteria** — This is your checklist. What must be true for the work to be considered "done"?
3. **Connect to your AI tool** — Link tashev-proof to your AI coding assistant (like Claude Code or Cursor)
4. **Start working** — Use your AI tool as you normally would

### Checking Your Work

When the AI says it's done:

1. **Run the verification** — Click "Check Work" in tashev-proof
2. **Wait for the results** — tashev-proof will analyze the work against your acceptance criteria
3. **Review the verdict** — You'll see a clear "SHIP IT" or "FIX IT" result
4. **View the evidence** — See exactly what passed and what failed, with supporting details

### Understanding Your Results

- **Green checkmark** — This criterion passed verification
- **Red X** — This criterion failed. Click it to see why
- **Yellow warning** — This criterion couldn't be fully verified. Review it manually
